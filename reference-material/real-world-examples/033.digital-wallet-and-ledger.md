# 033. Design a Digital Wallet & Ledger (Venmo / PayPal Balance)

## 1. Requirements
- Real-time peer-to-peer transfers and balance updates.
- 100% financial accuracy with zero discrepancies.

## 2. Architecture
- **Double-Entry Bookkeeping**: Every transaction writes two immutable ledger entries (Debit account A, Credit account B).
- **Partitioning**: Account balances sharded by user ID.
