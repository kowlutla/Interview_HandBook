# 031. Design a Hotel Booking & Reservation System (Booking.com / Airbnb)

## 1. Requirements
- Search available rooms by date range and location.
- Book rooms without double-booking conflicts.

## 2. Architecture
- **Inventory Locking**: Temporary hold on room inventory during checkout with expiration timeout (e.g., 15 minutes).
- **Database**: Relational database with strict isolation levels or distributed locking for booking creation.
