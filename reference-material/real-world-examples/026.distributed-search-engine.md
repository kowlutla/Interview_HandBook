# 026. Design a Distributed Search Engine (Elasticsearch-scale)

## 1. Requirements
- Full-text search across petabytes of documents with sub-second response times.
- Real-time indexing and distributed sharding.

## 2. Architecture
- **Inverted Index**: Maps terms to document IDs.
- **Shard & Replica Architecture**: Documents routed to shards via hash of ID; replicas ensure high availability.
