# 032. Design a Food Delivery Platform (UberEats / DoorDash)

## 1. Requirements
- Three-sided marketplace: Restaurants, Customers, and Couriers.
- Order placement, restaurant acceptance, driver dispatch, and real-time tracking.

## 2. Architecture
- **Order Management Service**: Coordinates order lifecycle across restaurant POS and courier dispatch.
- **Batching & Dispatch Engine**: Optimizes grouping multiple orders for single couriers.
