# 028. Design a Delivery Tracking System (DoorDash Driver Tracking)

## 1. Requirements
- Real-time live tracking of delivery couriers on customer map.
- Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) calculation and updates.

## 2. Architecture
- Driver app sends location pings over MQTT/WebSocket.
- Stream processor (Flink/Kafka) computes ETA adjustments and broadcasts updates to active customers viewing orders.
