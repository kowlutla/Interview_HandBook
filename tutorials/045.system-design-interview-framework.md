# 045. System Design Interview Framework Masterclass

## Overview
A structured, repeatable methodology for acing system design interviews at FAANG and top-tier technology companies. The framework provides clarity, structure, and depth under time constraints.

---

## The 4-Step Interview Framework (45-Minute Breakdown)

### Step 1: Understand the Problem & Establish Scope (5–10 mins)
Never jump straight into drawing boxes. Clarify requirements and constraints.
- **Functional Requirements**: What features are we building? Who are the users? What are the core write and read paths?
- **Non-Functional Requirements**: Scale (DAU/MAU, QPS peaks), latency SLAs, availability targets (e.g., 99.99%), consistency vs. availability tradeoffs (CAP theorem), durability, security.
- **Back-of-the-Envelope Estimations**: Calculate storage, bandwidth, and QPS requirements to guide technical choices (e.g., caching vs. database reads).

### Step 2: Propose High-Level Design (10–15 mins)
Sketch the core architecture and trace data flow.
- **Core Components**: Clients, Load Balancer, API Gateway, Microservices, Databases, Caches, Message Queues/Event Bus, Blob Storage, CDN.
- **API / Interface Design**: Define clean endpoints or protocol contracts (e.g., RESTful endpoints, GraphQL schemas, gRPC service definitions).
- **Data Model**: High-level schema or storage choice (SQL vs. NoSQL) for core entities.

### Step 3: Detailed Design & Deep Dives (10–15 mins)
Focus on bottlenecks, scaling challenges, and edge cases.
- **Scaling Bottlenecks**: Database sharding, read replicas, distributed caching strategies, leader election, partition tolerance.
- **Component Deep Dive**: Address specific hard problems relevant to the problem (e.g., deduplication in a crawler, rate limiting algorithms, conflict resolution in collaborative editors).
- **Fault Tolerance**: Redundancy, circuit breakers, rate limiting, retries with exponential backoff and jitter.

### Step 4: Wrap Up & Trade-Offs (3–5 mins)
Summarize your design and critically analyze its shortcomings.
- **Bottlenecks & Trade-Offs**: What are the failure modes? What did we sacrifice for speed or consistency?
- **Monitoring & Observability**: Metrics, logging, tracing, alerting.

---

## 2. Common Interview Anti-Patterns to Avoid
1. **The "Resume Match" Trap**: Trying to cram every trendy technology (Kafka, Kubernetes, Spark, GraphQL) into a simple CRUD problem without justification.
2. **Silent Thinking**: Designing in your head without communicating your rationale to the interviewer.
3. **Ignoring Edge Cases**: Failing to discuss concurrency control, race conditions, or network partitions.
4. **Poor Time Management**: Spending 30 minutes on database schemas and running out of time for high-level scaling and deep dives.
