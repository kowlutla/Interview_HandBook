# 003.5. PACELC Theorem

## Overview
The PACELC theorem extends the CAP theorem to provide a more complete characterization of distributed databases under both normal operations and network partitions.

## Theorem Definition
- **P**artition: If there is a **P**artition, how does the system choose between **A**vailability and **C**onsistency? ($PAC$)
- **E**lse (normal operation): When there is no partition, how does the system choose between **L**atency and **C**onsistency? ($ELC$)

## Examples
- **PC/EC (e.g., MongoDB)**: Chooses Consistency during partitions, and Consistency (higher latency) during normal operation.
- **PA/EL (e.g., DynamoDB, Cassandra)**: Chooses Availability during partitions, and Lower Latency during normal operation.
