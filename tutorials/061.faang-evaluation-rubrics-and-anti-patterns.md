# 055. FAANG Evaluation Rubrics & Anti-Patterns

## Overview
Understanding what interviewers look for and common pitfalls to avoid at senior levels (L5/L6/L7).

## Evaluation Dimensions
- **Requirements & Scope**: Did you ask clarifying questions?
- **High-Level Architecture**: Is the core design sound and complete?
- **Deep Dives & Technical Depth**: Can you justify technology choices and handle edge cases?
- **Trade-off Analysis**: Did you discuss pros/cons of your decisions?

## Common Anti-Patterns
- **Over-engineering**: Introducing complex microservices or Kafka for a simple CRUD app.
- **Deep-diving too early**: Designing database schemas before clarifying scale and traffic.
- **Silver bullet syndrome**: Claiming a tool solves all problems without drawbacks.
