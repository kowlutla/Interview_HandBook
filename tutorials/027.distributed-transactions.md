# 022. Distributed Transactions

## Overview
Distributed transactions ensure atomicity across multiple services or databases.

## Two-Phase Commit (2PC)
1. **Prepare Phase**: Coordinator asks all participants to prepare.
2. **Commit Phase**: If all prepare, coordinator sends commit; otherwise, rolls back.

## Saga Pattern
- Breaks transaction into sequence of local transactions.
- Each service performs its local transaction and publishes an event.
- Compensating transactions handle failures.

## Eventual Consistency
Accept that systems will be temporarily inconsistent after a failure, with reconciliation later.
