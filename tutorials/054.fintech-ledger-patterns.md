# 048. FinTech Ledger & Payment Patterns

## Overview
Designing robust, fault-tolerant financial transactions and immutable ledger systems.

## Key Principles
- **Double-Entry Bookkeeping**: Every transaction has equal and opposite debits and credits; the ledger must always balance.
- **Idempotency Keys**: Ensure retry requests don't result in duplicate charges.
- **Immutable Append-Only Ledger**: Never update historical financial records; instead, append compensating entries.
