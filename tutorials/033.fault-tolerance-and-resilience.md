# 028. Fault Tolerance and Resilience

## Overview
Building systems that withstand component failures without complete service disruption.

## Key Strategies
- **Redundancy**: N+1 or 2N redundancy for hardware and services.
- **Failover**: Automatic switching to backup systems on failure.
- **Graceful Degradation**: Reduce non-essential features under high load or failure.
- **Self-Healing**: Automatic restarts and state recovery.

## Single Points of Failure (SPOF)
Identifying and eliminating components whose failure takes down the entire system.
