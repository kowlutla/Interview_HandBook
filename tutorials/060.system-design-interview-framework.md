# 060. System Design Interview Framework

## Overview
A structured, methodical approach to tackling open-ended system design interview questions at top-tier tech companies. Success in system design interviews is not about memorizing specific architectures, but demonstrating a rigorous problem-solving process, deep technical judgment, and the ability to navigate trade-offs.

---

## The 45-Minute Interview Blueprint

### Step 1: Clarify Requirements & Scope (5-10 minutes)
Never jump straight into drawing boxes. Establish clear boundaries for the system.
- **Functional Requirements**: What features are we building? (e.g., posting a tweet, viewing a news feed, following users).
- **Non-Functional Requirements**: What are the scale and performance constraints?
  - High availability (e.g., 99.99% uptime).
  - Low latency (e.g., < 100ms read response time).
  - Scalability (e.g., 100M Daily Active Users).
  - Consistency vs. Availability trade-offs (CAP theorem).
- **Out of Scope**: Explicitly state what will *not* be covered to manage time effectively.

### Step 2: Back-of-the-Envelope Estimation (5 minutes)
Quickly quantify the scale to guide architectural choices.
- **Traffic Estimates**: Calculate Daily Active Users (DAU), average QPS, and peak QPS ($\text{Peak} \approx 2 \times \text{Average}$).
- **Storage Estimates**: Calculate daily and 5-year storage growth based on data payload sizes.
- **Network Bandwidth**: Ingress and egress throughput calculations ($QPS \times \text{Payload Size}$).

### Step 3: High-Level Design (10-15 minutes)
Sketch out the core components and data flow.
- **Clients**: Web, mobile, API clients.
- **Load Balancers & API Gateways**: TLS termination, rate limiting, authentication, and traffic routing.
- **Stateless Microservices**: Business logic layers scaled horizontally.
- **Data Stores**: Choosing between relational (SQL) databases, NoSQL key-value stores, document stores, or time-series databases.
- **Caching & Messaging**: Redis/Memcached for hot reads; Kafka/RabbitMQ for asynchronous processing.

### Step 4: Deep Dive & Bottlenecks (10-15 minutes)
Address specific scalability challenges and failure modes.
- **Database Scaling**: Sharding strategies, read replicas, and partitioning keys.
- **Caching Strategies**: Cache-aside, write-through, eviction policies (LRU/LFU), and cache invalidation.
- **Handling Failures**: Redundancy, circuit breakers, leader election, and disaster recovery.

### Step 5: Wrap Up & Review (3-5 minutes)
- Summarize the final architecture.
- Revisit trade-offs made (e.g., choosing eventual consistency over strong consistency for low latency).
- Discuss monitoring, logging, and alerting pipelines.
