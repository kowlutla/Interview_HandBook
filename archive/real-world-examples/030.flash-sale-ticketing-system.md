# 030. Design a Flash Sale / Ticketing System (Ticketmaster)

## 1. Requirements
- Handle massive flash traffic spikes (millions of users trying to buy limited inventory simultaneously).
- Prevent overselling and race conditions.

## 2. Architecture
- **Virtual Waiting Room**: Queue users before they hit checkout to smooth traffic spikes.
- **Atomic Inventory Decrement**: Redis Lua scripts or SQL row-level locks for atomic stock decrements.
- **Asynchronous Order Processing**: Successful reservations placed in Kafka queue for payment processing.
