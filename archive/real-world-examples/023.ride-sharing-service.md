# 023. Design a Ride-Sharing Service (Uber / Lyft)

## 1. Requirements
- Match riders with nearby drivers in real-time.
- Track driver locations continuously via GPS coordinates.
- Surge pricing calculations based on supply and demand.

## 2. Architecture
- **Location Ingestion Service**: High-throughput WebSocket/HTTP endpoint receiving driver GPS pings every 4 seconds.
- **Geospatial Index**: Quadtree, Geohash, or Uber H3 spatial indexing in Redis/PostGIS to find nearby drivers.
- **Dispatch Engine**: Matches request to optimal driver and handles ride lifecycle state machine.
