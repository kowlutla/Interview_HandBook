# 029. Design a Payment Gateway (Stripe / PayPal)

## 1. Requirements
- Process credit card and digital wallet transactions securely.
- Strict consistency, ACID compliance, and idempotency to prevent double-charging.
- Integration with external banking networks and fraud detection systems.

## 2. Architecture
- **Idempotency Key**: Client sends unique UUID with payment request; cached in Redis/DB to prevent duplicate executions on network retries.
- **Ledger Service**: Double-entry bookkeeping ledger guaranteeing balanced debits and credits.
- **State Machine**: Tracks payment lifecycle (Pending, Authorized, Captured, Settled, Refunded).
