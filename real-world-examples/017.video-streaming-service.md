# 017. Design a Video Streaming Service (YouTube / Netflix)

## 1. Requirements
- Video upload, transcoding into multiple resolutions/bitrates, and streaming.
- Adaptive bitrate streaming (HLS / DASH).
- Low buffering and high availability via CDN.

## 2. Architecture
- **Ingestion**: Upload service saves raw video to Blob Storage (S3).
- **Transcoding**: Event triggers distributed worker pool (FFmpeg on Kubernetes/AWS Batch) to encode video into 360p, 720p, 1080p, 4K.
- **Delivery**: CDN edge servers distribute chunks to clients.
