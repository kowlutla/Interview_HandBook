# 022. Design a Mass Email Campaign System

## 1. Requirements
- Send millions of personalized marketing emails reliably.
- Track opens, clicks, and bounce rates.
- Comply with rate limits of ISPs and spam regulations (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).

## 2. Architecture
- **Campaign Service**: Queues email jobs into partitioned Kafka topics.
- **Delivery Workers**: Rate-limited SMTP workers dispatch emails via SendGrid/SES.
- **Tracking Service**: Pixel tracking and redirect links record engagement events.
