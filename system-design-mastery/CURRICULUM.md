# Independent Curriculum and Coverage Matrix

## Curriculum design principles

This curriculum starts from the work an architect must do: define the problem, reason quantitatively, understand execution and communication, choose data semantics, reason about distribution and failure, then compose and operate the system. Technology names are included where they illuminate a general mechanism; they do not determine the chapter order.

The existing repository was consulted only to understand the separation between fundamentals and future real-world examples. Its numbering, chapter names, and sequence are not the organizing basis for this curriculum.

### External reference basis

This curriculum was cross-checked against broad, vendor-neutral architecture guidance and operational references rather than treated as a closed list. The [Azure Architecture Center's architecture styles](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/guide/architecture-styles) and [cloud design patterns](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/patterns) provide architecture-style and pattern taxonomies; the [AWS Well-Architected Framework](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/welcome.html) adds operational excellence, security, reliability, performance efficiency, cost optimization, and sustainability; the [Google SRE book](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/) emphasizes load balancing, overload, cascading failures, monitoring, and recovery; and the [OWASP API Security Top 10](https://api-security.owasp.org/editions/2023/en/0x00-header/) makes API-specific authorization and resource-abuse risks explicit. These are cross-checks, not a substitute for explaining the underlying mechanisms.
